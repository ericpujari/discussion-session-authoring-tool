# Not My Problem? (July 28 + 30) 🟢

## Title

- Not My Problem?

## Parent blurb

As players form Synthesis teams, and teams form Synthesis planets, the question of “who is responsible” for wins and losses can become muddy. In this week’s Discussion session, we put students in the position to explore responsibility in depth. From missed shots in sports, to the success of startups, to ensuring public safety, does responsibility fall on the individual, the leader, or the whole team? In each scenario, one of the options is “you” — the student. How often will they choose to take (or shift) responsibility? Either way, there are no right or wrong answers, just interesting conversations with kids from around the world.

Today’s session is about the concept of responsibility in a variety of different scenarios, and how often people will say, “Not me.”

 But it brings up a good question about Synthesis. There are no team captains here, no teachers telling you when to talk. So who is responsible to make sure the discussion is productive? That everyone is involved? That it stays on topic? Looks like we’re about to find out.

---

Who is really responsible when things go wrong or right? From missed shots in sports, to the success of startups, to ensuring public safety - the dynamics of responsibility can be complex. Is it on the individual, the leader, or the whole team? And in each scenario, one of the options will be YOU. Will you take personal responsibility, or shift the blame to others? Let’s look at the first scenario.

### Scenario: School Mistake 3-7

Your group is working on a school project. You find a major mistake the night before it's due. Who’s responsibility is it to make sure the project is ready?

You.
You discovered the issue and you’re able to fix it.

![noun-for-you-6973724 (3).webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-for-you-6973724_(3).webp)

The team leader.
They’re responsible for the project's success, no matter what.

![noun-team-leader-8196448.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-team-leader-8196448.webp)

The person who made the mistake.

They are accountable for their error, but they may not see it before tomorrow.

![noun-bad-1649029.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-bad-1649029.webp)

`challenge question`

- Which is more important: helping the team or letting the person learn from their mistakes?
- If you couldn’t fix the problem, what would you do? Does it matter that you CAN fix it?
- There’s only one error. But if there were multiple errors, what would you do?
- Is this about the outcome of the project? Or about whose job is whose?

`reflection question`

If this was a business and not a school project, would your answer be the same?

### Scenario 2: A Successful startup 7-11

You are the founder of a tech startup that unexpectedly skyrockets in success. Who should take the most responsibility for its rise?

You.
It was your vision that started the company, though you don’t work on the day-to-day details.

![noun-founder-6360668.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-founder-6360668.webp)

The marketers.
They crafted the brand's image and attracted customers effectively. After all, no customers, no business…

![noun-marketing-8369170.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-marketing-8369170.webp)

The customer service department

The startup was bumpy, but customers always felt taken care of, even when mistakes happened.

![noun-customer-service-5445646.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-customer-service-5445646.webp)

`challenge question`

- Does the person who had the idea deserve the success for how it’s built out?
- Could the company have survived without all three roles?
- What part of the business is most important: the idea, the growth, or the maintenance?
- Take a long-term approach — who will need to be most responsible going forward?

`reflection question`

Synthesis is a startup. Who deserves the responsibility for you being here?

### Scenario 3: An injury 11-15

Scenario 4: You see an individual accidentally injured right in front of a store. Who should take responsibility for the person’s wellbeing?

You.

A dozen other people are right there, but you have an opportunity to provide help or call for assistance.

![noun-for-you-6973724 (1).webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-for-you-6973724_(1).webp)

The store owner
They are part of the community and responsible for sidewalk safety on their property.

![noun-store-7149732.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-store-7149732.webp)

The police
They are assigned to patrol that part of the city, and responding to emergencies is their duty.

![noun-police-7956060.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-police-7956060.webp)

`challenge question`

- If you were the only person on the street, would that change your opinion? Why or why not?
- Does it matter what the store is and who runs it?
- Would you think differently if the injury was a sprained ankle vs. a broken leg?
- Do you assume that someone else will call for help? What if everyone assumes the same thing?

`reflection question`

If you were the injured person, what would you expect from others?

### Scenario 4: 🎴 Sports Loss 16-20

You miss a critical shot in the final seconds of a basketball game, and the team loses. Who should take responsibility for the loss?

You.
Even though every player missed some shots, yours was the one that mattered most at the end.

![noun-for-you-6973724 (1).webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-for-you-6973724_(1)%201.webp)

The coach.
They provide training and practice and should make sure players are prepared for pressure.

![noun-coach-8339769.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-coach-8339769.webp)

The team captain.
As a leader on the court, they could have made bigger plays earlier in the game.

![noun-cap-5801536.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-cap-5801536.webp)

`challenge question`

- What is the root cause of the loss? The shot? Or something deeper?
- Does this scenario feel more like an unlucky accident? Or a careless mistake?
- What makes a team captain “successful” or “unsuccessful”?
- Narrow down: is the responsibility for this loss more “on the court” or “off the court”?

`reflection question`

- Could there have been a fourth option here? If so, what would it be?

### Scenario 5: 🎴 raising child 20-24

Imagine you are a parent, and you expect your child to be a respectful member of society. Who is responsible to make sure that happens?

You.

Parents are the main source of values for their child. But do children always listen?

![noun-for-you-6973724.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-for-you-6973724.webp)

Your child.
No matter what they’re told, it’s your child that controls their own actions. 

![noun-child-1765613.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-child-1765613.webp)

Their friends.

Friends can be powerful motivators. If they encourage good behavior, it will stick. 

![noun-friend-8362660.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-friend-8362660.webp)

`challenge question`

- Do parents have as much influence as they think they do?
- Do you see yourself as responsible for your friends?
- Do you think the kid with the lollipop is going to grow up to be a respectful member of society?
- “It’s your child that controls their own actions.” Agree or disagree? Is that always true?
- If parents are responsible, should we blame all parents for their children’s behaviors?

`reflection question`

Who do you think is most responsible for you being the way you are?

### Scenario 6: 🎴 Synthesis 24-28

You’re in a game with a brand new teammate asking how to play Polis during a game that is really close. Who is responsible for helping the new player understand the game?

You.

Synthesis students help each other out, even if it means they might lose that game.

![noun-for-you-6973724 (4).webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-for-you-6973724_(4).webp)

The player.

The new player should learn by themselves, even if it’s less efficient than asking.

![noun-player-8064133.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-player-8064133.webp)

A Synthesis coach.

Coaches have the most game knowledge of all, and they have no team allegiance. 

![noun-coaching-8325934.webp](Not%20My%20Problem%20(July%2028%20+%2030)%20%F0%9F%9F%A2/noun-coaching-8325934.webp)

`challenge question`

- What happened to you when you were learning new games?
- How would you define the role of coaches at Synthesis?
- Are some Synthesis games easier/harder to learn than Polis?
- Which of these is the better long-term option?

`reflection question`

Who do you think is going to win the Polis Prize?
