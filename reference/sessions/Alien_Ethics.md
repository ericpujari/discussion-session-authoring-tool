# Alien Ethics (August 26+28)

## Title

- Alien Ethics

## Parent blurb

Our session on "Alien Ethics" challenges students to examine their morals and actions when interacting with extraterrestrial life. Inspired by popular science fiction works, each scenario provokes questions about disease, intelligence, and coexistence. How should we respond to these profound possibilities? No right or wrong answers here — just interesting conversations with kids from around the world.
