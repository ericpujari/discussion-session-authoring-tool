# Adapt or Persist? (March 24+26)

## Title

- Adapt or Persist?

### Event description

Is it better to stick with the plan? Or change course? It’s one of life’s toughest dilemmas, whether in friendships, entrepreneurship, or even Synthesis. Join the conversation and see for yourself. 

## Parent blurb

This week’s Discussion dives into one of life’s toughest dilemmas: when to stay the course and when to embrace change. From friendships to business ventures, art to lifelong dreams, the tension between sticking with what works and pivoting to new opportunities forces us to weigh risk against reward. No right or wrong answers here, just interesting conversations with kids from around the world.

### Scenario 1: Politics 2-6

You are running for student council, but your ideas aren’t as popular as you hoped. Should you stay consistent with your core values or adapt to shifting public opinion to gain more support?

Persist: stick with your same ideas.

![noun-straight-7860946.webp](Adapt%20or%20Persist%20(March%2024+26)/noun-straight-7860946.webp)

Adapt: shift policies to get support. 

![noun-diversion-3026572.webp](Adapt%20or%20Persist%20(March%2024+26)/noun-diversion-3026572.webp)

`Challenge`

In leadership, how important is staying true to your values?

Could a compromise between your values and public opinion be effective?

What are the risks of staying true to unpopular ideas?

How would you handle criticism if you flip-flopped to gain support?

Is the point to win the election? Or not?

### Scenario 5: Entrepreneurship 19-23

You started a small business selling custom bracelets, but sales have slowed down. Should you stick with your original product idea or pivot to make something new that customers seem to want?

Persist: keep your familiar brand the same.

### Reflection 27-28

- Adaptation: being able to pivot quickly.
