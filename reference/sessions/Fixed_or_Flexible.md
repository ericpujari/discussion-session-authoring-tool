# Fixed or Flexible? (April 7+9)

## Title

- Fixed or Flexible?

## Parent blurb

Sticking to the rules can bring discipline — or disaster. Adapting can lead to breakthroughs — or chaos. In life, knowing when to use structure and when to use flexibility is more of an art than a science. So this week, we give students practice with this tension. No right or wrong answers, just interesting conversations with kids from around the world.

### Scenario 1: Medical Treatment 2-6

A doctor is treating a patient using a standard, well-tested procedure. However, the patient is not responding immediately as expected, and a newer, less-tested approach is possible. In this situation, is it better to prioritize structure by following the accepted treatment or apply flexibility by trying the less-tested approach? Why?

Prioritize structure—proven methods have the best chance of success for patient safety.

![noun-rules-6622727.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-rules-6622727.webp)

Apply flexibility—adapting to the patient’s current needs may lead to a better outcome.

![noun-rule-2683012.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-rule-2683012.webp)

`Challenge Questions:`

How should doctors decide when to take risks and when to stay cautious?

 If the patient wants to try the new treatment, should the doctor always agree?

What happens if flexibility leads to more harm than good?

Is medicine more about following tested theories or adapting to new information?

`Reflection`

True or false: in general, doctors should not be experimental.

### Scenario 2: Bake-off 6-10

You are following a recipe to bake a cake for a contest, and you realize you are missing one ingredient. A trip to the store will take a lot of time. You have a possible substitute, but the results are uncertain. In this situation, is it better to rely on structure and follow the recipe exactly or use flexibility to make a change? Why?

Rely on structure—precision is important for a successful dish, so it’s best to follow the recipe.

![noun-rules-8127786.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-rules-8127786.webp)

Use flexibility—experimenting with substitutes could lead to something new and just as good.

![noun-flexibility-8226950.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-flexibility-8226950.webp)

`Challenge Questions:`

Are there some recipes where flexibility should never be used?

If you don’t follow recipes, what’s the point of writing them?

If people always followed recipes, would breakthroughs in food ever happen? 

`Reflection`

Who on your team has ever baked a cake without help?

### Scenario 3: Caretaking 10-14

You are babysitting your younger sibling, and your parent has set a strict bedtime. However, your sibling begs to stay up late to finish a fun activity. In this situation, is it better to follow the structured bedtime rule or show flexibility and allow an exception? Why?

Follow the structured bedtime rule—keeping routines consistent shows responsibility and reinforces rules.

![noun-clock-4402319.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-clock-4402319.webp)

Show flexibility—allowing one exception can make the evening more fun and strengthen your relationship.

![noun-clock-5395087.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-clock-5395087.webp)

`Challenge Questions:`

If you make an exception once, will it be harder to enforce the rule in the future?

Should a parent’s rule always be followed, or should it depend on the situation?

How will your choice affect your relationship with your sibling? With your parent? 

`Reflection`

Would your parent agree with your answer? (Go ask them!)

### Scenario 4: Writing a Book 15-19

You have carefully planned your book’s plot, characters, and themes. However, while writing, you come up with a new idea that could take the story in an exciting but unexpected direction. In this situation, is it better to rely on structure and follow the original outline or embrace flexibility and alter the story? Why?

Follow the original plan—staying structured will make the story more clear to readers.  

![noun-storyboard-4476080.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-storyboard-4476080.webp)

Embrace flexibility—exploring a new direction could lead to a more creative book.

![noun-storyboard-5652172.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-storyboard-5652172.webp)

`Challenge Questions:`

Could an unexpected idea be a distraction rather than an improvement?

If you abandon the original plan, how do you know when to stop making changes?

Do great stories come more from careful planning or inspired moments?

`Reflection`

Does anyone know someone who is writing a book?

### Scenario 5: Engineering 19-23

Your crew has been hired to build a bridge using a detailed, approved blueprint, but new data suggests a different design might be safer. Switching mid-project will cost your firm millions of dollars. In this situation, is it better to rely on structure by following the original blueprint or apply flexibility by making a change? Why?

Rely on structure — careful planning leads to safety, reliability, and efficiency.

![noun-blueprinting-6220486.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-blueprinting-6220486.webp)

Apply flexibility — using new information could create an even better bridge.

![noun-tactics-8251019.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-tactics-8251019.webp)

`Challenge Questions:`

When is it too late in a project to make major changes?

If the new design is untested, is it still worth the risk?

Should safety engineers follow standard procedures, or should they experiment?

How much is the money playing into your decision?

`Reflection` 

True or false: all new ideas come from failed experiments.

### Scenario 6: Social situations 23-27

A teammate was rude to you during a group conversation, and it hurt your feelings. Later, they act like nothing happened. You’re unsure whether to let it go or address it. In this situation, is it better to show flexibility and move on or rely on structure by setting clear boundaries? Why?

Show flexibility—everyone has bad days, and letting it go could keep the friendship strong.

![noun-relationship-7874611.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-relationship-7874611.webp)

Rely on structure—setting boundaries will show that respect matters in your friendship.

![noun-relationship-7982086.webp](Fixed%20or%20Flexible%20(April%207+9)/noun-relationship-7982086.webp)

`Challenge Questions:`

Is forgiving a friend a sign of strength? Or a mistake?

Does setting boundaries improve a friendship? Or risk harminig it?

What’s better in the long term: standing up for yourself or keeping the peace?

Do friendships need more structure or more flexibility to last?

`Reflection`

Would you do the same thing in a Synthesis session? Or not?
