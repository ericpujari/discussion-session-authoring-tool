# Deep Space (August 19+21)

## Title

- Deep Space

## Parent blurb

Why are adults and kids alike drawn to outer space? One reason could be the allure of the unknown: Earth problems are complex, of course, but they are also often “known knowns.” Deep space, on the other hand, include the “unknown unknowns” that capture our collective imagination. This week, six scenarios walk students though a deep space mission, from planning and preparation, to food and communication. The point? To practice thinking through the problems kids don’t usually consider; and in doing so, widen their future problem-solving capacity. Commencing countdown. Engines on.

The most important part of a discussion is making sure everyone has a chance to share ideas. After all, if only one person is talking in a group, that’s not a discussion. It’s a monologue.
