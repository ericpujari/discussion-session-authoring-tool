## Title

- The Definition of Success

### Event description

What does it mean to be successful? Is it having a lot of money, winning awards, or being famous? Or is it something quieter, like feeling proud of yourself, helping others, or finding happiness in what you do? Success means different things to different people. Let’s find out what your definition is.

Success is being supportive: help your teammate, even if it adds to the risk of failure.
